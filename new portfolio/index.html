Hillary Eland / GD STUDIOS

I turn ambitious ideas into things people can actually use.

I'm Hillary Eland, a 16-year-old software developer and founder of GD STUDIOS. I started coding at 14 and quickly became fascinated by the space where technology, design and imagination overlap.

My work spans front-end development, UI/UX, visual design, media concepts and experimental technology. I enjoy taking a rough idea, finding the interesting part of it, then shaping it into something polished, functional and memorable.

GD STUDIOS is my creative home for building modern digital products, interfaces, graphics and media projects that feel ambitious without losing the human touch.
